Tyrone Minor named a 2026 Community Builder.

Black Collective Foundation MN · 2026 Community Builders

Black Collective Foundation MN selected Tyrone Minor, Founder and CEO of The Mali Center, as one of 14 leaders in its 2026 Community Builders cohort. Community Builders guide how the Foundation invests in and shows up for Black communities across Minnesota.
